### Removal density (spot + sweepers)
- **White:** 8 instants + 7 sorceries = 15 removal/interaction spells (Swords to Plowshares, Wrath of God, Oblivion Ring, Condemn, etc.)
- **Blue:** 21 instants + 9 sorceries = 30 interaction spells (Counterspell, Mana Leak, Unsummon, Evacuation, etc.)
- **Black:** 8 instants + 15 sorceries = 23 removal spells (Doom Blade, Snuff Out, Toxic Deluge, Yahenni's Expertise, etc.)
- **Red:** 17 instants + 8 sorceries = 25 removal/burn spells (Lightning Bolt, Shock, Anger of the Gods, Starstorm, etc.)
- **Green:** 6 instants + 11 sorceries = 17 interaction spells (fight effects, artifact/enchantment removal)

**Removal assessment:** Blue and Red have the deepest interaction suites. Black and White are well-stocked. Green has appropriate fight/naturalize effects. All colors can interact meaningfully.

### Key observations
1. **Excellent color balance** — monocolor sections are within 2 cards of each other
2. **Healthy curve** — supports aggro, midrange, and control strategies
3. **Strong mana base** — ABUR duals, shocks, Pathways, surveylands, triomes provide excellent fixing for 2-3 color decks
4. **Adequate removal** — every color has plausible interaction
5. **Creature-forward design** — 42% creatures supports board-based gameplay and package synergies (Aristocrats, Tokens, ETB Value, etc.)
