# The Archive

- **CubeCobra shortId:** thearchive
- **CubeCobra link:** https://cubecobra.com/cube/list/thearchive
- **Size:** 540
- **Format:** draft

## Identity

A nostalgia cube spanning Magic's 30+ year history, featuring at least 3 cards from every Standard-legal set ever printed (excluding Universes Beyond). Designed to evoke a trip down memory lane, mixing iconic bangers with obscure cards from sets that rarely see play. Features 15+ packages instead of rigid archetypes, allowing creative deck-building and skillful self-expression.

## Power level & philosophy

Mid-power. Power level intentionally constrained so older cards can compete alongside newer cards. Touchstones: Serra Angel, Mahamoti Djinn, Sengir Vampire, Shivan Dragon, Force of Nature — these Alpha/Revised iconics should feel playable in most decks. Exciting power outliers included to keep things fresh, but balanced across decks. Avoids most "word soup" cards that are difficult to parse.

## Design constraints

- **Standard-legal sets only** — every card must have been Standard-legal at some point in Magic's history
- **Minimum 3 cards per set** — each Standard set represented must contribute at least 3 cards to the cube
- **Supplemental exceptions** — Chronicles (1995), Modern Horizons 1 (2019), Modern Horizons 2 (2021), Modern Horizons 3 (2024) are included despite not being Standard sets
- **Banned sets** — Arabian Nights (ATQ), Adventures in the Forgotten Realms (AFR), all official Universes Beyond sets (2025+)

## Hard no's

- Universes Beyond cards and sets considered UB-adjacent (AFR, ARN)
