# Cube Ideas

Unrefined cube ideas jotted down for future use. Newest first. Promoted ideas
link to their cube directory when bootstrapped — move the deep design work to
the cube's `backburner.md` at that point, leaving the idea line here for
history.

## 2026-08-03

- **Keruga's day out** — cube built around Keruga's companion restriction: only cards with mana value 3+ (plus lands). Early game comes from "secret twos" — 1- and 2-drop effects that live as activated abilities on cards in hand (cycling, evasion, loots, etc.).
- **Invasion of SMOLworld** — story-telling cube of small (SMOL) creatures, with a few giant monstrosities as deliberate power outliers. Tuning lever: removal weak enough that fatties can't be easily dealt with, but present enough that they don't run away with every game.
- **Oddworld** — cube containing only cards with odd mana values (lands excluded). Secret twos and other ways to use mana in even increments fill the gaps.
