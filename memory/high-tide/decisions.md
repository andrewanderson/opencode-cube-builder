# Decisions

Newest first. Each entry: date, change, rationale.

## 2026-08-30
- Land base target revised: **55 Islands / 85 nonbasic (140 total)** confirmed as the new locked target, replacing the 2026-08-03 80/60 split — mock drafts showed off-color decks needed more fixing density than planned. The "abundant Islands, contested spells" feel held up despite the lower Island count.
- Opposition, Upheaval, Control Magic, and Treachery cut to maybeboard (not in the 432) after playtester feedback (Discord) flagged them as un-fun — repeatable lock effects / one-sided theft. Draw-go control survives as an archetype, re-scoped to a classic UW/UB shell (counterspells + spot removal + board wipes) instead of lock pieces.
- One peer flagged Force of Will + Daze as too strong for the format; kept in deliberately. Watch item for the first human draft, not acting on it yet.
- Golgari/Sultai (BG) stretch pair locked: **Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord** (`{B}{B}{G}{G}`, true non-blue, hardest cast in the format) + **Sidisi, Brood Tyrant** (`{1}{B}{G}{U}`) — fills the gap in the off-color-gold roster (the other 5 pairs — BR/GR/GW/BW/RW — already had 2 each; BG had zero). Sidisi's mill+token engine feeds Jarad's graveyard-count/sac-drain and pushes toward the Self-mill package's existing win-cons (Laboratory Maniac, Jace, Wielder of Mysteries). User to determine the 2-card cut to make room.
- Corrected the off-color-gold roster: of the existing 10, 2 (Jeskai Ascendancy, Alela, Artful Provocateur) actually touch blue per Scryfall despite showing as non-blue in CubeCobra's Color field — that field is manually overridden in this cube (blue stripped for swimlane display; see identity.md caveat), not literal mana cost. Both remain valid stretch-tier members under the existing mana-cost-equivalence rule (a 3-color-including-U card is the same difficulty tier as its 2-color non-blue equivalent).

## 2026-08-03
- Land base targets locked: 140 total lands (32% of 432), 80 Islands, 60 non-basics — Comparable desert cubes (Drought 480-card, Down 432-card snow cube) run 34–35% lands; 32% is intentionally tight to reward disciplined drafting. 80 Islands (~57% of lands, ~19% of cube) ensures they flood packs and wheel freely; 60 non-basic slots reserved for Ux duals and colorless utility.
- Cube bootstrapped — 432 cards, 18-card packs, blue-prison desert cube with Islands as only basics and blue-touched or colorless fixing for other colors.
