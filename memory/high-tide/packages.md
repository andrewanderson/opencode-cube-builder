# Packages

A package is a self-contained bundle of cards that supports a specific play pattern without committing to a full archetype. Decks blend packages. This file reflects the cube's current state only — see `decisions.md` for history and rationale, `backburner.md` for pending/unconfirmed changes.

## Other notable threads (not full packages)

These don't have an enabler/payoff structure and aren't draftable pillars, but they're deliberate and worth tracking so they aren't mistaken for random goodstuff.

- **Blue hate (G)** — resilience tools that let a deck fight through counterspells/blue interaction. 10 of green's ~38 nonland cards carry this text (over a quarter of the color) — see `identity.md`'s "Blue as oxygen" section for the full breakdown. Cards: Skylasher, Karoo Meerkat, Sporeweb Weaver, Veil of Summer, Surrak Elusive Hunter, Destiny Spinner, Balustrade Wurm, Seedtime, Eyes of the Wisent, Arasta of the Endless Web.
- **Tap-down value (WU)** — tap opponents' creatures to trigger payoff effects. A couple of build-arounds, not a pillar. Enabler: Diversion Unit. Payoffs: Hylda of the Icy Crown, Verity Circle.
