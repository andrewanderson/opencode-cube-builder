# Packages

A package is a self-contained bundle of cards that supports a specific play pattern without committing to a full archetype. Decks blend packages.

| Package | Play pattern | Colors | Enablers | Payoffs | Support level | Pairs with | Competes with |
|---------|--------------|--------|----------|---------|---------------|------------|---------------|
| High Tide storm | Chain untap effects and cheap cantrips into a storm count, closing via an alt win-con or storm spell | Mono-U | Peregrine Drake, Cloud of Faeries, Frantic Search | Thassa's Oracle, Laboratory Maniac | Weak — namesake card (High Tide), Turnabout, Palinchron not yet in cube | Spellslinger, Draw-go control | — |
| Tinker / artifact ramp | Ramp into or cheat a colorless bomb into play | Mono-U or Ux | Emry, Lurker of the Loch; Sai, Master Thopterist | Portal to Phyrexia, Wurmcoil Engine, Urza Prince of Kroog | Moderate | Reanimator, Polymorph | — |
| Standstill / Blueggro | Deploy a cheap evasive clock, hold up cheap counters to protect it | Mono-U or Ux tempo | Spell Pierce, Miscalculation | Slither Blade, Faerie Vandal, Pestermite | Weak — Standstill, Force of Will, Daze not yet in cube | Ninjas, Spellslinger | Draw-go control (different speed of blue deck) |
| Ninjas | Attack with a cheap evader, ninjutsu in a value creature for a burst of value | UB | Satoru Umezawa; cheap evaders shared with Standstill | Fallen Shinobi, Kaito Bane of Nightmares, Thousand-Faced Shadow, Ingenious Infiltrator | Strong | Standstill/Blueggro, Self-mill | — |
| Spellslinger | Cast a critical mass of cheap instants/sorceries to trigger token/damage/draw payoffs | Ux (esp. UR), mono-U | Cube's cantrip suite | Murmuring Mystic, Triskaidekaphile, Crackling Drake, Third Path Iconoclast, Enduring Curiosity | Strong | High Tide storm, Draw-go control | — |
| Draw-go control | Counter/answer everything, draw cards, win with a shared finisher | Ux or mono-U | Cube's counterspell/removal suite | Curator of Destinies, Frost Titan, Fact or Fiction, Access Denied, Sphinx of Foresight | Strong | Tinker, Reanimator, Steal (shared finishers) | Standstill/Blueggro |
| Self-mill | Fill your own graveyard fast, leverage it for reanimation, delve, or an alt win-con | UB or mono-U | Thought Scour, Stream of Thought | Cemetery Illuminator, Devourer of Memory, Perpetual Timepiece | Strong | Mill, Reanimator | — |
| Mill (aggressive) | Race the opponent's deck instead of their life total | UB | Ruin Crab, Merfolk Secretkeeper, Picklock Prankster | Mindwrack Harpy, The Mindskinner | Strong once Glimpse/Cacophony/Laughter are procured | Self-mill | — |
| Island-matters / Islandwalk | Reward drafting Islands (which nearly every deck does) with evasion, draw, or scaling stats | Cross-color, strongest in GU | Islands themselves | Cold-Eyed Selkie, Coastal Bulwark, Flow of Knowledge, Mu Yanling Sky Dancer, Archway of Innovation | Moderate — thin pool so far, worth prioritizing over time | All Ux archetypes | — |
| Reanimator | Fill the yard, then bring a fatty back under your control | UB | Unearth, Nether Traitor | The Eldest Reborn, Virtue of Persistence, Forum Necroscribe | Moderate | Self-mill, Tinker (shared fatty targets) | — |
| Opposition / tokens | Generate expendable tokens, tap the opponent's board/lands down every turn | Ux midrange/control | Hard Evidence, Nadir Kraken | Sai Master Thopterist, Rite of Replication, Etherium Spinner | Weak — Opposition itself (the tap-lock namesake) not yet in cube | Polymorph | — |
| Polymorph | Sac a cheap/disposable creature, flip it into the biggest thing in your library | Ux | Hard Evidence, Nadir Kraken | Precursor Golem, Wurmcoil Engine, Portal to Phyrexia | Weak — Polymorph, Proteus Staff (the enablers) not yet in cube; currently just a pile of flip targets | Opposition/tokens, Tinker | — |
| Upheaval | Float mana through a mass-bounce, redeploy from a stronger position | Ux ramp | Mind Stone, Fellwar Stone, Guardian Idol, Lotus Petal | Wurmcoil Engine (redeploy target) | Weak/unresolved — Upheaval itself not yet in cube; anti-synergy with own land base already flagged, cut-vs-keep undecided | — | Own land base (bounces your Islands too) |
| Steal / cast opponent's stuff | One-shot theft: take their creature/artifact or cast their card, don't lock them out permanently | UB | — | Hostage Taker, Lullmage's Domination, Thieving Skydiver, Fallen Shinobi, The Eldest Reborn | Moderate — Bribery, Control Magic, Treachery, Gilded Drake (the named payoffs) not yet in cube | Reanimator, Ninjas, Draw-go control | — |
