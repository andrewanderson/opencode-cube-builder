## 2026-08-03

### Archetypes from Gemini brainstorm (to evaluate one at a time):

**High-priority / strong fit:**
- **High Tide storm** — namesake archetype. High Tide + untap spells (Turnabout, Frantic Search, Palinchron) + cantrips → storm kill (Brain Freeze) or infinite turns (Time Walk, Temporal Manipulation). Mono-U combo that rewards drafting Islands.
- **Tinker / artifact ramp** — cheat colorless fatties (Blightsteel Colossus, Portal to Phyrexia, Cityscape Leveler) via Tinker, Urza, Emry. Mono-U or Ux, doesn't need off-blue lands.
- **Opposition / tokens** — Opposition + token generators (Sai, Third Path Iconoclast, Hard Evidence) to tap down opponent lands/creatures. Ux midrange/control lock.
- **Vedalken Shackles package** — Shackles scales with Island count (perfect for this cube). Add Treachery, Control Magic, Bribery as "steal your stuff" package. Mono-U or Ux control.
- **Standstill / Blueggro** — drop 1-drop (Delver, Ledger Shredder, creature-land), cast Standstill, ride to victory with free counterspells (Force of Will, Daze). Mono-U or Ux tempo.

**Medium-priority / needs reframing:**
- **Polymorph** — token generators that aren't creatures (Hard Evidence, Shark Typhoon, planeswalkers) + Polymorph/Proteus Staff → flip into sole fatty. Make sure payoffs overlap with other archetypes (reanimator, ramp).
- **Upheaval** — float mana, bounce everything, redeploy threat. Anti-synergy with Islands (bounces your lands); only works if floating artifact mana. Needs testing or cut.

**Low-priority / niche:**
- **Stifle-Nought** — counter ETB triggers (Phyrexian Dreadnought, Uro, Kroxa) with Stifle/Tale's End/Dress Down. Less relevant without fetchlands. Dress Down is flexible; pure Stifle is narrow.
- **Self-mill / Lab Man** — Laboratory Maniac / Thassa's Oracle + self-mill engines (Mesmeric Orb, Thought Scour). Parasitic; 1-2 win cons + light support is enough.
- **Donate / Illusions** — Illusions of Grandeur + Donate, or Grid Monitor + Donate. Cute Ux control finisher, not a pillar.

**Skip:**
- **Stasis lock** — miserable to play against, requires heavy setup (Chronatog, Forsaken City, Capsize). Could be 1-of "if you draft the pieces" but don't build a package.
